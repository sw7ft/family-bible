Privacy

Family Bible · SW7FT · last updated 21 September 2026

Family Bible does not collect, sell, or transmit personal information. There is no account and no analytics.

What stays on the phone

Reading place, bookmarks, profiles, journal entries, comments, and pictures you attach are stored only on this device (and in that device’s backup if you use iCloud or a computer backup). SW7FT never receives them.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to attach a picture to a note, comment, devotion, or prayer. Copies are saved inside the app. We do not upload them.

Network

The app does not need a network connection to read or write. Opening this privacy page or the GitHub repository uses your browser, not the app.

Children

The app is a Bible reader. It is appropriate for a family. It does not ask for a name, email, or location.
